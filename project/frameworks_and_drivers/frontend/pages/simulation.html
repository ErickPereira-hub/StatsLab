Statistical Simulations

You can decide which simulation you’d like to explore, experiencing your interests and goals. If you already have a specific model in mind, simply use the search bar below to locate it quickly. Whether you’re experimenting, learning, or analyzing, the tools here are designed to make your journey through statistical simulations both intuitive and engaging.

Normal Distribution

This is the Normal Distribution option. Here, you can evaluate probabilities using a normal distribution model and visualize them through the Gaussian curve that represents it.

Descriptive Results

This is a region where you can fetch descriptive results of your dataset, including mean, median, standard deviation, and much more.

Poisson Distribution

This is the Poisson distribution option. Here, you can calculate the probability of an event occurring multiple times within a given interval of time or space, and visualize the results through a chart.

Polynomial Regression

This tool allows you to fit a polynomial curve to your data with a chosen degree. It not only calculates the best-fitting polynomial but also displays it graphically, making it easy to visualize trends and patterns in your dataset.

Binomial Distribution

This is the Binomial Distribution option. In this section, you can evaluate the probability of achieving a certain number of successes within a given range, based on the total number of attempts and the probability of the event that is being analyzed.

Linear Regression

This tool offers the line curve that best fits your data. It not only calculates the best-fitting line but also displays it graphically, making it easy to visualize trends and patterns in your dataset.
