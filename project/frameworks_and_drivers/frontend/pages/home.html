Welcome to StatsLab

This is the ultimate interactive simulator designed to bridge the gap between complex data and actionable insight. Whether you need to upload custom datasets to uncover hidden probabilities and advanced metrics, or want to master core statistical concepts through real-time dynamic modeling, StatsLab transforms passive learning into an active, hands-on experience.

About the project

This open-source project is built to support students, teachers, and scientists by making data exploration, learning, and teaching entirely confortable. It empowers students to master new statistical concepts through clear, intuitive visuals, while enabling teachers to deliver highly engaging lessons using custom visual aids. Scientists can also have advantages by using the platform to analyze and visualize complex datasets efficiently. By taking raw input data, the tool converts numbers into precise, static images and dynamic, interactive animations designed to explain complex ideas.
